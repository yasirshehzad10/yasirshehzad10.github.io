About

Muhammad Yasir

I'm a chemical engineer from Multan, based in Islamabad. I did my BSc at NFC Institute of Engineering and Technology, where I scored 98 out of 100 on the final comprehensive exam, the highest in my cohort.

I started out freelancing. For two years I built process simulations for about twenty clients, mostly gas turbine and LNG models, and wrote up the results myself. Since 2021 I have worked as a systems engineer in Islamabad, running process and combustion simulations for engineering programmes.

The papers came out of collaborations. The carbon capture and liquid hydrogen work is with colleagues at Durham University. The LNG work was done with NUST in Islamabad and Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University in Saudi Arabia. I am first author on all four.

Right now I'm adding a machine learning layer on top of the rigorous models. A surrogate trained on simulator runs can answer quickly what the full model answers slowly, as long as it is checked against the physics. I'm testing that first on the liquid hydrogen model. If you work on related problems, write to me.
